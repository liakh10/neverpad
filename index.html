How Never Pad works

Not a token launchpad. A launchpad built around things not repeating.

01
What happened

Describe the event that already occurred. Subject, value, date, source. This becomes the reference and is fixed forever.

02
Define the never

What counts as it happening again. Same thing, the next level, or your own condition. The coin is right as long as it never does.

03
Create the coin

A coin is minted, paired with the never condition, with a counter that starts at the reference date.

04
Wait

Every day without a repeat the counter goes up. If it repeats, the counter resets to zero and everyone can see it.

Questions
Is this a prediction market?

No. A prediction market pays when something happens. Here the coin is paired with it not happening. The counter is the product.

What if it happens again?

The counter resets to zero. The coin stays where it is, the reset is written into it, and everyone who held it saw the number.

What if nothing ever happened?

Then there is nothing to never repeat. The reference event is required.

Are the example markets real?

No. Markets tagged EXAMPLE show how the product reads. Coins you launch on this page are simulated and live in your browser.

Where does the counter come from?

From the reference date. It is a clock, not an oracle. Repeats in the examples are not tracked automatically.
